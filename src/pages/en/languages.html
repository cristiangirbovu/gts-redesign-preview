Combinations of two foreign languages

A translation can also be made directly from one foreign language into another, without using Romanian as an intermediate language, when the translator is authorized for both languages.

In certain situations, especially for translations that require notarial legalization, the translation must be done in two stages, using Romanian as an intermediate language. In this case, the cost and delivery time are calculated for both stages.

Before starting work, we check which option applies to your documents and inform you of the procedure, cost and delivery time.
