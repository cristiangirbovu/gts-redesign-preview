About us

With experience in translation since 2004, we provide translation and interpretation services for individuals, companies and institutions. We work with translators authorized by the Ministry of Justice and manage projects across a wide range of languages and specialist fields.

We have our own offices in Bucharest and Buzău and provide our services both on site and online, for clients throughout the country.

Our quality standards

Every translation is carried out with attention to meaning, terminology, consistency and the presentation of the final document.

Faithfulness to the original

We render the information in the source document accurately and completely, without omissions or changes in meaning.

Correct terminology

We use the terminology specific to the field of each document, for a translation that is correct, precise and adapted to its context.

Consistency and linguistic accuracy

We ensure consistent terminology and compliance with the rules of grammar, spelling and punctuation.

Preserving the original format

We preserve, as far as possible, the structure of the document – tables, lists, numbering, notes and other formatting elements.
