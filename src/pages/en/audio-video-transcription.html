Audio-video transcription

{{> trust-strip}}

We convert the content of audio and video recordings into written text that is clear and easy to use, faithfully preserving the information in the original material. We produce transcriptions in the language of the recording and, on request, the content can also be translated into the language you need.

What we transcribe

We can process a wide range of audio and video recordings, such as:

  • interviews and conversations;
  • intercepted recordings;
  • internal and business meetings;
  • conferences, seminars and webinars;
  • telephone recordings;
  • courses and educational materials;
  • presentations and speeches;
  • podcasts;
  • video materials and multimedia content;
  • recordings intended for legal or administrative proceedings.

Transcription adapted to the purpose of the document

Depending on your needs, the transcription can reproduce the full content of the recording or be edited into a clear, structured form, with the speakers identified and the dialogue organized.
