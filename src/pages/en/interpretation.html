Interpretation

Interpretation services for situations in which the presence of an authorized interpreter is required.

Main types of interpretation

  • Simultaneous interpretation – the interpretation is delivered in real time, at the same time as the speaker. It is used especially at conferences, congresses, events and presentations and may require dedicated technical equipment.
  • Consecutive interpretation – the interpreter listens to part of the speech, may take notes, and then renders the message in the other language during the speaker's pauses. It is suitable for meetings, negotiations, discussions and events with a small number of participants.
  • Chuchotage (whispered interpretation) – the interpreter renders the speech in a whisper, simultaneously, for one or a few people, without interrupting the speaker.
  • Liaison interpretation – the interpreter facilitates communication between people who speak different languages, translating alternately in both directions. It is frequently used at business meetings, negotiations, visits and professional discussions.
  • Remote interpretation – the service can be provided by telephone or video call, without the interpreter and the participants being in the same location. It is a practical solution for online meetings, professional discussions, hearings before authorities and other situations in which physical presence is not required.

Legal and institutional interpretation

Business interpretation

We provide interpretation services for business meetings, negotiations, visits by international partners and other professional events.
