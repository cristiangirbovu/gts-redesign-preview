Video subtitling

{{> trust-strip}}

We translate and subtitle video materials in various languages, adapting the text so that the original message is rendered clearly, naturally and correctly.

Subtitling involves both translating the spoken content and adapting the text to the rhythm of the dialogue and synchronizing it with the video.

Subtitling services can be provided for:

  • films and video materials;
  • interviews and documentaries;
  • conferences, seminars and webinars;
  • courses and educational materials;
  • presentations and corporate materials;
  • tutorials and training materials;
  • advertisements and promotional materials;
  • videos for websites and online platforms;
  • other audio-video materials.

We adapt the text to the pace of speech and the length of each line and synchronize it with the video, so that the subtitles appear at the right moment and can be read easily.

We can produce subtitles directly from the video or starting from an existing transcription, and at the end we deliver the subtitle file in the format you request.
