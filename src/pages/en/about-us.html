Who we are

We started in 2004, in Buzău, as a local authorized translation office. Over time we opened a second office in Bucharest, in the Pipera area, for clients in the capital and in northern Ilfov.

GTS Company's employed translators are experienced authorized translators who perform translations in various fields: legal, technical, medical, financial, commercial, economic and literary.

What sets us apart

Real offices

You can come in with your document, in Buzău or in Pipera. We do not work exclusively by courier, although we do so when it is more convenient for you.

Procedure, not improvisation

We hold the ISO 9001:2015 Certificate for written and oral translation activities, in accordance with the SR EN ISO 9001:2015 standard.

Response within 2 hours

We do not keep you waiting a day to find out a price. During business hours, the quote goes out within two hours.

We tell you what you need

Clients often ask for "a translation", but the institution wants something else. We ask where you will submit the document and tell you the correct form from the start.

ISO 9001:2015 certification

Certification is not just a logo on a website. The certificate covers both written and oral translation, including interpretation, and confirms that the office has implemented and maintains a quality management system. Details on the ISO accreditation page.
