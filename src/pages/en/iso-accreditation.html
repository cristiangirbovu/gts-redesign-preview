ISO 9001:2015 Certification

GTS Company holds the Certificate ISO 9001:2015 for written and oral translation activities, which certifies that it has implemented and maintains a quality management system which fulfils the requirements of standard SR EN ISO 9001:2015.

What the certification covers. Both written and oral translation, that is, including interpretation services, not just documents.

What it means for you

ISO 9001:2015 is the international standard for quality management systems. The certification confirms that the office has implemented such a system and maintains it, being audited periodically by an independent certification body.

For you, as a client, it means working with an office whose processes are documented and audited, not merely declared.

Who performs the translations

GTS Company's employed translators are experienced authorized translators who can perform translations in various fields: legal, technical, medical, financial, commercial, economic and literary.
