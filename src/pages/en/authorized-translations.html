Authorized translations

{{> trust-strip}}

What is an authorized translation?

An authorized translation is a translation performed by a translator authorized by the Ministry of Justice, who certifies, by signature and stamp, the accuracy of the translation and its faithfulness to the document presented.

What is the difference between an authorized translation and a legalized translation?

An authorized translation is certified by the signature and stamp of the translator authorized by the Ministry of Justice. A legalized translation additionally involves the legalization of the translator's signature by a public notary.
