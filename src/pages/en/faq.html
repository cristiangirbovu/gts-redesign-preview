General questions

Who performs my translation?

GTS Company's employed translators are authorized translators with experience who can perform translations in various fields: legal, technical, medical, financial, commercial, economic, literary.

Translation types

What is an authorized translation?

An authorized translation is a translation performed by a translator authorized by the Ministry of Justice, signed and stamped by them. Details on the authorized translations page.

What is a legalized translation?

A legalized translation is an authorized translation with the mention of the translator's signature legalization by a public notary. Details on the legalized translations page.

What is an apostille?

The apostille is a resolution, a note, an observation, applied to a petition or an official act. It is actually a square-shaped stamp (with a 9 cm side), identical in all member states of the Hague Convention and which is applied directly to the document to be legalized, or on an extension of it. Details on the apostille and superlegalization page.

What does "translation direction" mean?

Translation direction represents the chosen linguistic combination:

  • Simple translation: from a foreign language into Romanian
  • Retroversion: from Romanian into any language
  • Double translation: from one foreign language into another foreign language
How is the number of pages calculated?

A page is calculated at a number of 1800 characters without spaces (can be calculated using the Wordcount option in Microsoft Word). Civil status documents, certificates and attestations are charged as 1 page.

Deadlines and payment

How much time is needed to complete a translation?

We set the deadline together. You tell us when you need the document, and in the quote we confirm whether we can meet it and under what conditions. We do not work with standard deadlines, but with the deadline you need.

What is the price of a translation?

To be able to estimate the price of a translation as accurately as possible, we recommend that you request a price quote from us. The quote is free and you receive it within 2 hours, during business hours.

How do I pay?

Payment methods accepted by GTS Company are:

  • Cash payment at one of the GTS Company offices
  • Payment by bank transfer to the GTS Company account

Documents and delivery

Do I need the original document?

For an authorized translation, a clear scanned copy is sufficient. The original or a certified copy is required for notarial legalization, because the notary must see the document from which the translation was made.

Do you also work with clients from other cities?

Yes. Documents travel by courier, and the translation can be delivered to you by courier anywhere in the country. For legalization, the physical original is required.

Can I come directly to the office?

Yes, to both offices, during business hours. For interpretation, however, an appointment in advance is required.
