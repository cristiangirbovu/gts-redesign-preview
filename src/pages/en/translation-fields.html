Translation fields

{{> trust-strip}}
Legal and administrative translations

Contracts, articles of association, certificates issued by the Trade Register, court judgments and decisions, powers of attorney, declarations, notarial deeds, civil status certificates, criminal records, documents issued by public authorities and institutions, documents for immigration and citizenship, case files and other legal or administrative documents.

Medical and pharmaceutical translations

Medical reports and letters, laboratory tests, hospital discharge papers, medical records, investigation results, medical certificates, pharmaceutical documentation, package leaflets, clinical studies and documents relating to medical devices.

Technical and industrial translations

Technical manuals, user instructions, technical data sheets, specifications, documentation for machinery and equipment, projects, diagrams, and documents in the fields of construction, engineering, the automotive industry and energy.

Translations for tenders and public procurement

Tender specifications, award documentation, technical and financial bids, qualification documents, the ESPD (DUAE), declarations, certificates, authorizations, contracts, bidder documents and the technical documentation required to take part in public procurement procedures.

Economic, financial and banking translations

Financial statements, balance sheets, financial reports, bank statements, income certificates, accounting documents, audits, tax documents, activity reports, and banking and insurance documentation.

Commercial and business translations

Commercial contracts, invoices, offers, business correspondence, company presentations, reports, internal policies and procedures, and import and export documents.

IT and software translations

Websites, applications, online platforms, software interfaces, user manuals, IT documentation, support materials, privacy policies and digital content.

Marketing and advertising translations

Websites, brochures, catalogues, presentations, advertisements, promotional materials, advertising campaigns, newsletters, press releases and content for online and social media.

Scientific and academic translations

Scientific articles and papers, research papers, studies, bachelor's and master's theses, doctoral theses, academic materials, presentations and documents intended for specialist publications.

Literary and editorial translations

Books, literary texts, articles, essays, short stories, manuscripts, editorial materials and other texts intended for publication.

Automotive translations

Registration certificates, vehicle identity cards, certificates of conformity (COC), sale-purchase contracts, purchase invoices, proof-of-origin documents, customs documents, technical data sheets and specifications, vehicle manuals and other documents required for vehicle registration or use.
